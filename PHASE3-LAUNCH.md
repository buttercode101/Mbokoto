# Phase 3 limited-pilot brief

## Release boundary
Limited beta / partner review. Mbokoto is not an emergency dispatch service and must never be represented as one.

## Positioning
When someone has not arrived or may be in danger, Mbokoto helps a trusted person organise the first hours: consent, last-known timeline, preservation requests, a station pack and place-hold requests. Local/shareable artifacts are not proof that SAPS, a business or a responder received or acted on them.

## ICP
Primary user: trusted family/friend supporting a missing or threatened person. Primary buyer: none initially. Early adopters: university/community/GBVF organisations willing to review and pilot safely.

## Activation
Create a case -> review timeline -> create station pack or preservation request without false delivery state.

## North star
Safely completed first-hours case packs reviewed by an authorised user/partner, with no false-delivery incidents.

## Sustainability
Core first-hours usefulness stays free. Explore grants, university/NGO pilots and CSR sponsorship only after partner review.

## Experiments
1. Safety/GBVF expert review of workflow and wording. Success: 3 structured reviews. 30 days.
2. University/NGO tabletop pilot using fictional cases only. Success: users complete flow and identify trust/recovery gaps. 30 days.
3. Missing-person reporting checklist comparison against SAPS guidance. Success: no missing mandatory reporting information within supported scope.

## Partnership message
Mbokoto is a local-first prototype for organising the first hours when someone is missing or threatened. It does not dispatch police or private responders. I am looking for a safety/GBVF organisation to review the consent, missing-person timeline, preservation-request and station-pack workflow using fictional cases before broader public promotion.

## Evidence log
No claimed users, rescues, dispatches, partnerships or safety outcomes without confirmed evidence.
